Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok Alert Bot ("the Bot") handles data when it operates in your Discord server.

1. Data We Collect

  • Server configuration data: server (guild) ID, the Discord channel IDs you choose for notifications, and the TikTok usernames you ask the Bot to watch. Stored in a local watchlist.json file on the machine running the Bot.
  • Public TikTok data: publicly visible posts, captions, and live-stream status of watched accounts, used solely to generate notifications.
  • Bot token: the Discord bot token, stored in a local environment file (.env) on the machine running the Bot.

The Bot does not collect message history, personal messages, emails, phone numbers, or payment information. It only reads the slash commands you send it.

2. How We Use Data

Collected data is used only to:

  • Deliver the notifications you requested (new posts, live streams)
  • Maintain your server's watch list

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data. Data is only processed by the third parties required to operate the Bot:

  • Discord — to deliver messages (Discord's Privacy Policy applies)
  • TikTok — the Bot requests public profile information (TikTok's Privacy Policy applies)
  • Optional proxy / signing services — if you configure a proxy, requests are routed through it as directed by the server operator

4. Data Storage and Retention

Watch-list data is stored locally on the machine hosting the Bot and retained until you remove it with the /unwatch command, remove the Bot from your server, or the server operator deletes the data.

5. Data Removal

You can remove your server's data at any time by running /unwatch for each watched account or by kicking the Bot from your server. Server operators may delete watchlist.json to erase all stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
